# PhysiQuest privacy policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026 (version 1.5)_

PhysiQuest is a physics-learning app made by Mugilan (contact: mugilstrange@gmail.com).

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** PhysiQuest has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.

- Your progress (XP, badges, finished activities and your best mock-test score) is saved **only on your own device**, inside the app's storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
- Uninstalling the app, or using **Badges → Reset my progress**, deletes it.
- The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
- The app makes a random Device ID (six letters and numbers) and keeps it on your device. It is used only to make Premium unlock codes work on your device.

## Payments (direct-download app only)

In the version downloaded from GitHub, Premium can be paid for by UPI. Tapping **Pay with GPay / UPI** opens your own UPI app (such as Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm); the payment happens entirely inside that app, and PhysiQuest never sees your UPI PIN, card or bank details. If you choose to send your payment details, your own email app opens with the plan, the UPI transaction ID (UTR) and your Device ID filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send. The Google Play version does not take payments.

## Feedback

Bug reports and topic ideas are sent only if you choose to, from your own email app, to mugilstrange@gmail.com.

## Internet use

All lessons, simulations and fonts are built into the app, so it works offline. If you tap a link to an outside website, that page opens in your web browser and that website's own privacy policy applies.

## Children

PhysiQuest follows the NCERT physics books from Class 6 to Class 12, so some learners may be about 11 years old. The app collects no personal information from anyone, shows no ads, and has no sign-in or chat. A parent or teacher should help younger students with any payment.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
