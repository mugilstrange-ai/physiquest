# PhysiQuest privacy policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026 (app version 1.19)_

PhysiQuest is a physics-learning app made by Mugilan. Contact for questions, corrections and complaints: **mugilstrange@gmail.com**.

## In short

- You can use PhysiQuest **without an account**. Then nothing about you leaves your device.
- If you **sign in with Google**, we keep only what is needed to run your account, save your progress and manage Premium.
- We **never sell** your details, show **no ads**, use **no analytics or tracking**, and build **no profiles** of students.
- You can **edit or delete** your account at any time inside the app.

## Without an account

Your progress (XP, badges, finished activities and mock-test scores) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app, or **Badges → Reset my progress**, deletes it. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## With an account (optional)

When you choose **Continue with Google**, Google tells PhysiQuest your name, your email address and a Google account ID. PhysiQuest never sees your Google password.

You then fill in a short form. We store:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Name, class, city or town, state | To run your account and show the right content |
| Mobile number (optional) | To contact you about a payment if needed |
| Whether you are 18 or older | Students under 18 need a parent's agreement |
| For students under 18: parent or guardian's name and mobile number, and that they agreed | To record the parent's agreement and, if needed, confirm a payment with them |
| Your progress | So it is not lost if you change or lose your phone |
| Your Premium plan and its end date | So Premium works on every phone you sign in to |

We do **not** ask for or store your home address or date of birth.

These records are stored with **Google Firebase**, a service run by Google. Each person can read only their own records; the PhysiQuest owner can read them to manage accounts and payments.

## Payments

Premium is paid for on **Razorpay's** secure payment page, by UPI, card, net banking or wallet. Razorpay (Razorpay Software Private Limited) handles the payment: PhysiQuest never sees your card, UPI PIN or bank details. What you enter on Razorpay's page is covered by Razorpay's own privacy policy: <https://razorpay.com/privacy/>.

When you tap **Upgrade**, the PhysiQuest payment server (which runs on Cloudflare and keeps no records itself) checks your sign-in and gives Razorpay your name, your email address, the plan and the price, so Razorpay can show them on the payment page and send you its receipt.

When Razorpay confirms the payment, we store the payment ID, the plan, the amount, how you paid (for example UPI or card), the date and your email with your account, and switch Premium on. Nothing is charged again automatically.

Payment requests sent with a UPI transaction ID (UTR) in earlier versions of the app are kept as they were: the UTR, the plan, the amount, the date, your name and your email.

The Google Play version of PhysiQuest does not take payments.

## Your choices and rights

- **See and correct** your details: **Account → Edit details**.
- **Delete your account**: **Account → Delete my account**. This removes your details, your saved progress and your sign-in. Records of payments (payment ID or UTR, plan, amount, date, name and email) are kept for as long as the law requires for financial records, and are used for nothing else.
- **Withdraw a parent's agreement**: the parent or the student can delete the account in the app, or email us and we will delete it.
- Any other request or complaint: email **mugilstrange@gmail.com**. We aim to reply within 7 days.

## Children

PhysiQuest follows the NCERT physics books from Class 6 to Class 12, so many learners are under 18. Students under 18 can create an account only with a parent or guardian's agreement, and we collect only what is listed above. There is no chat, no public profile, no advertising and no tracking. A parent or teacher should help younger students with any payment.

## Feedback

Bug reports and topic ideas are sent only if you choose to, from your own email app, to mugilstrange@gmail.com. A bug report may include the app version, your phone's browser version and the last few error messages from the app, to help fix the problem.

## Internet use

Lessons, simulations and fonts are built into the app, so it works offline. The internet is used only for the optional account. If you tap a link to an outside website, it opens in your web browser and that website's own privacy policy applies.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
